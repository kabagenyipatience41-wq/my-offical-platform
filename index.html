About Kabagenyi Patience

Hello! My name is Kabagenyi Patience, and this blog is my little home on the web. I started it because I have a lot of thoughts that deserve more than a notes app, and because I believe sharing honestly is one of the best ways to grow.

I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor of Information Systems and Technology, where I'm learning how technology, data and people come together to solve real problems. I love asking questions, building things and turning everyday moments into stories.

Here you'll find what I'm studying, what I'm figuring out, and sometimes what went delightfully wrong along the way, from late-night assignments to little lessons about tech and life.

My name means patience, and I'm still learning to live up to it — one good day at a time. If something I write makes you smile, think, or want to write back, then this blog is doing its job.

Thoughts 20 Oct 2026 · 4 min

What my name keeps teaching me

Being named Patience comes with jokes, expectations and a few good lessons.

People love to comment on my name. “You must be very patient!” Honestly? Some days I'm not. But having a name that's also a quiet instruction has made me pause more often than I otherwise would.

Patience, I've found, isn't waiting without feeling anything. It's staying kind to yourself while things take the time they take.

Learning 11 Oct 2026 · 5 min

Learning in public is scary (and worth it)

As an IT student, I decided to share the messy middle, not just the finished result.

It's tempting to only show what's polished. But the most useful tutorials and notes I've read during my Information Systems and Technology studies were written by people brave enough to say “I didn't get this at first.”

So that's my promise here: honest notes, mistakes included. If I'm confused, you'll probably find out.

Everyday 29 Sep 2026 · 3 min

A list of very small joys

Warm tea, a good song at the right moment, and other things that fix a Tuesday.

Big goals get all the attention, but my days are really held together by small things: a message from a friend, sunlight on the floor, the first sip of something warm.

I've started writing three small joys down each evening. It turns out noticing them is a skill, and it gets stronger with practice.

Thoughts 15 Sep 2026 · 4 min

Why I finally started this blog

I kept saying “one day.” Then I opened a blank file instead.

For a long time, I told myself I'd start writing when I had more time, better ideas, or a nicer website. None of those showed up. So I made the site first and the excuses second.

Welcome to the result. It's colourful, a little imperfect, and completely mine.
